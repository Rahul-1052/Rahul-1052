### What is Stratify?

Creators can see **views, likes, comments, and watch metrics**, but those numbers don't necessarily explain *why* one piece of content performs differently from another.

Stratify is being built to investigate that gap.

Instead of relying only on performance metrics, Stratify analyzes observable characteristics of content and compares them across videos to identify patterns associated with stronger and weaker performance.

The goal is not to generate generic AI advice. It is to produce recommendations that can be traced back to **observable evidence**.

### How It Works

```text
Acquire Content
      ↓
Observe Video + Metadata
      ↓
Extract Features
      ↓
Build Evidence
      ↓
Compare Videos
      ↓
Discover Patterns
      ↓
Evaluate Confidence
      ↓
Generate Experiments

```
